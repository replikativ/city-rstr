Sources and denominators

Resident and occupied-dwelling counts use the 2021 Census at dissemination-area level. Intact households come from the Vancouver metropolitan hierarchical census sample. Age shares use all residents. Tenure and size use private households. Income and labour use private-household residents aged 15 or older. Income is individual total income for 2020. Employment and high income by gender are held out of fitting. Rows with unavailable or empty census denominators are omitted from both sides of aggregate comparisons.
