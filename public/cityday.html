    Movement and map evidence

    Counts include every synthetic resident in the selected study area. Dots show a fixed sample of residents at known places or travelling on mapped streets and scheduled routes. Click a dot to inspect that resident. Journeys without a mapped position still contribute to the travelling count. In street mode, pale green dots show pedestrians and pink dots show cyclists within 250 m, drawn from the full roster. Each pure walking or cycling trip must fit its diary time on the corresponding street network. The walking ceiling is 150 m/min and the cycling ceiling is 220 m/min.
