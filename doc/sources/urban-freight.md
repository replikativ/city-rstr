# Goods movement and urban metabolism

## Deferred implementation plan

Revisit freight after the current Vancouver and Stuttgart simulation and explorer implementation is complete.

1. Pilot food deliveries and waste collections on the UBC academic campus. Record which supplier, receiver, commodity and facility relationships the sources establish and which require inference.
2. Connect establishment inventories and orders to shipments, vehicle tours and curb stops. Keep tonnes, vehicle crossings, deliveries and expenditure as separate measures. Account separately for goods consumed locally and goods passing through the region.
3. Test heavy-truck approach shares against an independent count period. Test stop frequency and dwell against sampled receiving sites. Report uncertainty for payloads and customer assignments. Use Stuttgart’s cordon, harbour and loading-zone evidence to test which model components transfer across cities.

## Research and sources

Research note for Vancouver and Stuttgart. The proposed unit of observation is a physical establishment. One company can run several establishments, a carrier can serve several buyers on one tour, and the consignee can differ from the buyer. Record evidence separately for a facility, a commercial relationship, a shipment and an observed vehicle stop. Give each a source, date, geographic scope, units and evidence status.

A campus food pilot could couple purchases, consumption, surplus and organics collection through a daily stock balance. Fit annual totals and plausible order frequencies, consolidate orders into tours subject to payload and time windows, and generate a distribution of delivery stops. Use dated aerial images to locate docks and service paths. Survey vehicle class, arrival/departure and coarse load at sampled receiving sites. Reserve separate sites or weekdays for checking stop count, dwell and time-of-day distributions. A gate crossing, a delivered pallet and a dollar of retail spending each need their own observation model.

The local inventory contains campus geometry, establishments, roads and several traffic counters. Existing resident commute inputs do not identify freight payloads or commercial counterparties. Additional source leads from the research audit:

- The [Canadian Freight Analysis Framework](https://www150.statcan.gc.ca/t1/tbl1/en/tv.action?pid=2310014201) gives historical aggregate commodity flows between broad origin and destination areas by mode. The [regional truck network](https://catalogue.data.gov.bc.ca/dataset/translink-truck-routes) constrains assignment. [G3's North Vancouver terminal](https://www.g3.ca/en/our-network/g3-terminal-vancouver/) is a documented rail-to-ship grain facility, illustrating why gateway throughput and local consumption need separate accounts.
- [UBC waste operations](https://planning.ubc.ca/sustainability/sustainability-action-plans/zero-waste-action-plan/recycling-and-waste-operations) documents the collection of academic-campus garbage and transport to the Vancouver Waste Transfer Station. [Campus waste statistics](https://sustain.ubc.ca/dashboard/waste) and [Metro Vancouver's regional report](https://metrovancouver.org/services/solid-waste/Documents/solid-waste-management-annual-summary-2023.pdf) give independent totals at different scales. Those geographic and material boundaries must be reconciled before fitting.
- [Stuttgart traffic counts](https://opendata.stuttgart.de/dataset/ergebnisse-der-verkehrszaehlungen) and [Stuttgart harbour throughput](https://opendata.stuttgart.de/dataset/warenumschlag-im-hafen-stuttgart) give separate checks on road movements and port mass. The [German transport forecast](https://www.bmv.de/verkehrsprognose) provides modeled commodity flows and an explicitly dated base case. The [city's truck through-traffic rules](https://www.stuttgart.de/leben/mobilitaet/logistik/lkw-durchfahrtsverbot) distinguish through traffic from local deliveries. The [Smart Zone final report](https://www.stuttgart.de/medien/ibs/221019-smart-zone-final.pdf) supplies local curb observations for testing dwell and occupancy assumptions.
